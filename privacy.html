Privacy policy

Personal Backup Utility is for the account owner’s private use.

Access and use

The utility requests access only to Google Drive files created by or explicitly opened with it. File content and metadata are used solely to store, verify, retrieve and manage the owner’s backups.

Protection and sharing

Backup content is encrypted before upload. Credentials and backup content are not published on this website. The utility does not sell user data or use it for advertising or AI training. Google processes data as needed to provide Drive.

Control and deletion

The owner controls backup retention and deletion and can revoke access through Google Account settings. Revoking access does not automatically delete existing backups.

This website and contact

This static website has no forms or analytics scripts. Its hosting provider, GitHub Pages, may process standard access logs. For questions, use the operator’s support email displayed on the Google consent screen.
